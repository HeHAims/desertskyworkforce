# WorkFlowOS Client Kickoff & Discovery Phase

Prepared by Symbiotic AI LLC

## Purpose

This document explains what happens when a customer approves the WorkFlowOS project and is ready to move forward.

The demo and phone notification shown during the introduction are part of the proof of concept. They demonstrate the direction of the platform: jobs, milestones, employee updates, inventory awareness, and owner notifications.

## Project Offer

Symbiotic AI LLC will provide a customized licensed version of WorkFlowOS for the customer's internal business operations.

The first project price is $7,000.

This price covers the licensed platform setup, company workflow configuration, core app customization, employee roles, inventory tracking, milestone notifications, deployment setup, and basic onboarding.

## Payment Schedule

To begin the full build, the customer pays a 50% deposit.

- Total project price: $7,000
- Deposit to start: $3,500
- Final payment before launch: $3,500

The deposit allows Symbiotic AI LLC to move from proof of concept into the full working system.

- Customized WorkFlowOS workspace setup

- Job and task tracking
- Milestone tracking
- Owner and manager notification setup
- Simple inventory tracking

## Discovery Phase

The first step after payment is a focused Discovery Phase. This protects both sides from building the wrong system or agreeing to vague requirements.

During discovery, Symbiotic AI LLC will map:

- Employee roles
- Manager responsibilities
- Owner/admin permissions
- Job stages
- Milestone names
- Inventory categories
- Notification rules
- Spanish/English employee wording
- Devices used by the team
- Any required reports or approval steps

Discovery creates the final project blueprint for the first working version.

## Estimated Timeline

- Discovery and workflow mapping: 2-4 business days
- Working MVP build: 14-21 business days
- Client review, adjustments, and launch setup: 5-10 business days

Expected first version delivery: about 21-30 days after deposit and completion of required client information.

More advanced features may be scheduled as phase two.

- Owner dashboard

- Milestone changes

## Notifications

The system is designed so the owner can receive alerts when important events occur, including:

- New job created
- Milestone changed
- Job blocked
- Inventory marked low or missing
- Job ready for review
- Job completed

Initial notifications may use SMS. Push notifications can be added as a future phase depending on the final deployment and device strategy.

## Licensing And Ownership

Symbiotic AI LLC provides a customized licensed version of its business workflow platform.

Customer receives the right to use the customized application for its internal business operations while the account is active and in good standing.

Customer owns its business data, uploaded content, branding, product information, customer records, and images.

Symbiotic AI LLC retains ownership of the underlying platform, source code, reusable modules, templates, architecture, developer tools, and governance technology.

The platform may be customized with Customer's preferred name, branding, workflows, and content, but customization does not transfer ownership of the underlying software.

## Hardware And Third-Party Costs

Third-party service costs are also separate unless stated in writing. These may include hosting, database services, SMS/phone notifications, email delivery, domains, and storage.
